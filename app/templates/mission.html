Our Mission

To make high-quality chess accessible, inspiring, and visible across the East Bay.

The EBCS aims to make chess accessible for the East Bay community and spread the love for chess through a friendly environment, helping bring the community together.

What we stand for

East Bay Chess Society exists to raise the quality of local chess events and to create a lasting home for players who want more than a casual meetup. We want to support a culture where beginners can learn safely, families can participate comfortably, and ambitious players can test themselves in well-run tournaments.

Our club is also about visibility. By building a strong public presence, we make it easier for community partners, schools, and event organizers to see chess as a serious and positive force in the region.

What success looks like

  • Reliable monthly events with clear registration and communication
  • Strong turnout from both beginner and advanced players
  • Partnerships with local institutions and community spaces
  • A reputation for professionalism, kindness, and excellent organization
