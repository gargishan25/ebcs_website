Dedicated to making chess accessible for all.

The East Bay Chess Society (EBCS) is a student-led organization dedicated to making chess more accessible for enthusiasts and competitors, while also supporting players attempting to ease back into the game. We aim to develop the game of the younger generation while helping them build a genuine connection with chess.
