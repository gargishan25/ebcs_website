2026 East Bay Rapid & Blitz

Overview

Date: October 3, 2026

Location: Centerville Community Center: 3355 Country Dr, Fremont CA, 94536.

Registration closes: September 26, 2026

Sections: u1000, 1000-1499, 1500+ - If a participant does not have a USCF quick rating and wishes to play in the Rapid tournament (or does not have a blitz rating and wishes to play in the Blitz tournament), they will be put into a section based on their USCF regular rating. However, the tournament will still affect the blitz/quick rating (depending on the event).

Capacity: u1000 - 18 players, 1000-1499 - 16 players, 1500+ - 16 players.

Cost & Requirements

Cost: $5 for Rapid, $5 for Blitz, or $10 for both.

Requirements: Active USCF membership (please make sure it isn't expired).

Rules: Half-point byes must be requested before the first round begins. To request a play-up, email eastbaychesssociety@gmail.com if you're within 100 points of the cutoff for the next section.

Schedule

Time controls: Rapid 10+5 (5 rounds), Blitz 3+2 (6 rounds).
