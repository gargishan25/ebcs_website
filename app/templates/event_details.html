2026 East Bay Rapid & Blitz

Overview

Date: November 7, 2026

Location: RSM Fremont Mission, 39995 Mission Blvd, Fremont, CA 94539

Sections: u1000, 1000-1499, 1500+ - If a participant does not have a USCF quick rating and wishes to play in the Rapid tournament (or does not have a blitz rating and wishes to play in the Blitz tournament), they will be put into a section based on their USCF regular rating. However, the tournament will still affect the blitz/quick rating (depending on the event).

Capacity: u1000 - 40 players, 1000-1499 - 40 players, 1500+ - 40 players.

Requirements

Registration closes: October 31, 2026

Cost: $5 for Rapid, $5 for Blitz, or $5 for both (free tournament).

Eligibility: Must have an Active USCF membership (please make sure it isn't expired).

Rules: Half-point byes must be requested before the first round begins. To request a play-up, email eastbaychesssociety@gmail.com if you're within 100 points of the cutoff for the next section.

Note: Boards, pieces, and clocks will be provided. Please make sure to bring food and water, as well as a notation sheet and pen/pencil if you are interested (not recommended for Blitz).

Schedule

Time controls: Rapid 10+5 (8 rounds), Blitz 3+2 (8 rounds).
