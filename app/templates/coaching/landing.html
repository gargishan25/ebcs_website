How it works

Each step is handled by a person, not an algorithm. Our software ranks the possible matches, and EBCS staff make every final pairing.

01

Tell us about you

Your level, what you want to work on, and the hours you're free each week. Takes about three minutes.

02

Get matched

We weigh shared availability, rating gap and coaching focus, then pair you with one coach.

03

Meet weekly

Online, by video call. Your coach plans each session, files notes afterwards and sets homework.

04

Watch yourself improve

Your skill map shows where you've grown across tactics, openings, endgames and more.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. EBCS is a student-led initiative and our coaches volunteer their time. There are no fees, ever.

Who are the coaches?

High-school players with a verified USCF or online rating. Every applicant is reviewed and approved by EBCS staff before taking on learners.

How do you keep younger learners safe?

Learners under 18 need parent or guardian consent. All sessions are online. All coach messages stay on the platform, where EBCS staff can review them. Read our guidelines.

How long until I get a coach?

It depends on how many coaches are free at your times. The more hours you mark as available, the faster we can match you. You'll see your place in the queue on your dashboard.

How strong do I need to be to coach?

There's no fixed cutoff. We look for a solid rating, clear communication and reliability. Your learners just need you to be meaningfully stronger than them.

Do you track coaching hours?

Yes. Each session you report is logged automatically, so you and EBCS can both see how many hours you've spent supporting learners.
