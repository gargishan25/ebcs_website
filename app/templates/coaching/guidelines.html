EBCS Coaching

Community guidelines

EBCS Coaching brings together people of every age. These rules keep it safe, respectful and worth everyone's time. They apply to learners, coaches and families.

Everyone

  • Be kind and patient. Everyone here is learning, the coaches included.
  • Show up on time. If you can't make a session, cancel it on the platform at least 24 hours ahead.
  • No harassment, hateful language, or pressure to share personal information.

Communication

  • All messages between coaches and learners stay on the EBCS platform. EBCS staff can read every thread.
  • Coaches never ask learners for phone numbers, social media accounts or private contact details.
  • If a message makes you uncomfortable, use the Report link next to it, or email eastbaychesssociety@gmail.com.

Sessions

  • Online sessions use a meeting link shared on the platform.
  • Learners under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent to join. Their guardian's details are visible only to EBCS staff.

Coaches

  • File a session report within 48 hours. Your logged hours come from these reports.
  • Only log hours you actually worked. EBCS staff check every entry.
  • Tell EBCS staff right away about any safety concern.

Breaking these guidelines can lead to removal from the program. Questions? Email eastbaychesssociety@gmail.com.
