Code of conduct

  • Keep all communication with learners on the EBCS platform. Don't exchange personal phone numbers or social media.
  • Show up on time. If you have to cancel, give at least 24 hours' notice.
  • File a session report within 48 hours. That report is how your hours get logged.
  • Be patient and encouraging, and tell EBCS staff right away if anything feels wrong.
